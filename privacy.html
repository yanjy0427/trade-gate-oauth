Privacy Policy — trade-gate

Last updated: 26 October 2026

trade-gate is a private application used by a single individual (the operator) to manage that person own business email. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

1. Who operates it

Yan Jianyun, contactable at yanjy0227@gmail.com. The application runs on the operator own computer.

2. What data is accessed

The application uses Google OAuth with two Gmail permissions:

  • gmail.readonly — to read messages in the operator own mailbox.
  • gmail.send — to send messages from that same mailbox.

No other Google data (Contacts, Drive, Calendar, etc.) is requested or accessed.

3. How data is used

Email content is used solely to draft, review and send business correspondence from the same account. Message content may be processed locally by the operator own scripts for classification and record keeping.

4. Storage

OAuth refresh tokens and any local records are stored on the operator own machine, protected by file permissions. No Gmail data is uploaded to any third-party server.

5. Sharing

Gmail data is never sold, rented, transferred or disclosed to third parties. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models. Google user data is not used by humans for any purpose other than operating this mailbox.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records are kept only as long as needed for business correspondence. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page; local tokens are then deleted.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

trade-gate use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
