trade-gate

trade-gate is a private, single-operator email tool. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does

It connects to the Gmail account of its only operator (Yan Jianyun, yanjy0227@gmail.com) to read incoming business email and to send replies and transactional messages from that same account.

Data access

  • Reads only messages in the operator own Gmail mailbox.
  • Sends email only from that same mailbox, to recipients the operator specifies.
  • Stores no data on any third-party server.
  • Does not sell, share, or transfer Gmail data to any third party.
  • Does not use Gmail data for advertising or to train AI models.

Privacy policy

The full privacy policy is at privacy.html.

Summary: no one reads the mailbox except the operator; access tokens are stored locally on the operator own machine; data is never shared with third parties.
